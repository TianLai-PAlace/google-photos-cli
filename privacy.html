Privacy Policy

Data Access

This application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access Google Photos with the account owner's permission.

Data Usage

Google user data is used solely to upload photos, create albums, and manage application-created content in Google Photos.

Data Storage and Protection

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the user's device. Photos are transferred directly to Google Photos using HTTPS.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI training.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may delete locally stored credentials, revoke application access through their Google Account settings, and delete uploaded photos through Google Photos.
